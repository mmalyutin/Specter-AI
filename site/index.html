How it works

From download to first answer in about two minutes.

Step 1

Install & connect

A 2-minute setup wizard connects your OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Codex plan key. It's stored encrypted on your machine.

Step 2

Press a hotkey

Ctrl+Enter captures your screen, transcribes meeting audio, and sends both as context with your question.

Step 3

Get answers only you see

Responses stream into a translucent overlay that Zoom, Meet, and Teams screen sharing cannot capture.

Installation

Per-platform notes and trust prompts.

  1. Download specter-setup.exe and run it.
  2. If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This prompt appears while builds are unsigned and goes away once signed releases ship.
  3. Follow the setup wizard — that's it.
  1. Download specter-mac-arm64.zip (Apple Silicon) or specter-mac-x64.zip (Intel) and unzip it.
  2. Drag Specter AI to Applications.
  3. First launch: right-click the app → Open (bypasses Gatekeeper for unsigned builds).
  4. Grant Screen Recording and Microphone permissions when prompted (System Settings → Privacy & Security).
  1. Download specter-linux-x64.AppImage, make it executable (chmod +x), and run it.
  2. Or install specter-linux-x64.deb with your package manager.
  3. X11 works best; screen-capture exclusion is limited on Wayland.

FAQ

Is using Specter AI during meetings or exams allowed?

That depends on the rules of your meeting, employer, school, or exam provider — many prohibit assistance tools. Specter AI is intended for legitimate use: accessibility, note-taking, translation, and remembering your own prep. You are responsible for following the rules that apply to you.

Where does my data go?

Everything stays on your machine except the AI API call you trigger, which sends extracted text (and optional screenshots) to your chosen provider with your own key. No telemetry, no analytics, no servers of ours.

Is it really free?

The app is free and open source (MIT). You bring your own AI access: OpenRouter has free models, Groq transcription has a free tier, and Codex-plan users need no API key at all.
